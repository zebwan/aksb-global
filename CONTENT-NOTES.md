## 📸 Photos worth asking the client for

Ranked by how much they would improve the site:

1. **Road furniture on site** — thermoplastic line marking being laid, a finished chevron or
   rumble strip run, signage being erected, road studs or flexible posts installed. This is
   scope 01 and currently has no imagery at all.
2. **Civil & structure** — a gabion or slope protection wall, fencing, wall fencing. Scope 03,
   also with no imagery.
3. **A sharp re-shoot of HFST.** The existing one is unusable and it is a flagship scope.
4. **Sealant joint and bridge deck repair** — currently covered only by the elastomeric and
   asphaltic plug photos.
5. **Reclamite and Techscreed** — both named on the site with no photo behind them.
6. **People and plant.** There is not a single photo of the crew, a branded truck, the line
   marking machine or the office. Contractor sites live on these; right now the site shows
   finished road surfaces but never the company doing the work.
7. **Landscape / wide shots.** Everything supplied is a portrait-ish phone snap. Wide shots
   crop far better into the hero grid and page banners.

Practical note for the client: shoot landscape, hold still, and **turn the camera's timestamp
watermark off** — three of the six usable photos needed cropping to remove it, which costs
image area every time.
